Data Privacy and Research Consent

MicroCount STEVision is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of information submitted through the system while supporting legitimate scientific and educational research.

This notice explains how information, uploaded microscope images, and research-related data may be collected, processed, stored, protected, and used.

1. Legal Basis

The collection and processing of personal information associated with MicroCount STEVision shall be conducted with due regard to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its applicable implementing rules and regulations.

The Data Privacy Act establishes principles including transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality for the processing of personal information.

Important: MicroCount STEVision is a research and image-analysis system. The privacy notice does not replace the requirements of the research institution, school, ethics review process, or other applicable policies.

2. Information That May Be Collected

Depending on how the system is used, MicroCount STEVision may process the following research-related information:

  • School or institution name
  • Section or research group information
  • Water source information
  • Water condition
  • Number of days the sample was stored
  • Microscope magnification
  • Sample identification information
  • Number of microscope images
  • Uploaded microscope images
  • Image-analysis measurements
  • Suspected particle counts
  • Particle morphology classifications
  • Research results generated by the system
Data minimization: Users should not submit unnecessary personal information when using MicroCount STEVision. Only information reasonably necessary for the stated research purpose should be provided.

3. Uploaded Microscope Images

Microscope images uploaded to MicroCount STEVision are processed for the purpose of image analysis and suspected microplastic particle quantification.

The system may analyze the images to identify visible particle candidates, estimate particle counts, classify particle morphology, and generate research results.

Uploaded images should contain only information necessary for the research. Users should avoid including faces, identification cards, names, addresses, contact information, or other unnecessary personal information in microscope images.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

Information submitted to MicroCount STEVision may be processed for the following legitimate research purposes:

  • Analyzing microscope images of water samples.
  • Quantifying suspected microplastic particles.
  • Classifying suspected particles according to the morphology criteria used in the research.
  • Comparing computational image-analysis results with manual counting.
  • Evaluating the accuracy and consistency of the MicroCount system.
  • Preparing research reports, presentations, and academic outputs.
  • Improving the research methodology and documentation of the MicroCount system.

Information should not be processed for a purpose that is incompatible with the purpose communicated to the data subject, except where otherwise permitted by applicable law.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Research information and uploaded images should be stored only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the declared research purpose or other applicable requirements.

Where temporary browser storage is used by MicroCount STEVision, information may be stored locally in the user's browser, including browser storage mechanisms such as local storage.

Users should understand that clearing browser data, changing browsers, or using another device may remove locally stored research information.

Research retention: The research team should establish and document an appropriate retention period consistent with the approved research protocol and applicable institutional requirements.

6. Data Security and Protection

Appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards should be applied to protect research information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Security practices include:

  • Limiting access to research information to authorized members of the research team.
  • Avoiding unnecessary collection of personal information.
  • Keeping research files in appropriately secured storage.
  • Using password protection or access controls where applicable.
  • Avoiding unauthorized sharing of uploaded images and research information.
  • Removing or securely deleting information when it is no longer necessary for the declared research purpose, subject to applicable retention requirements.

7. Research Confidentiality

Research information will be handled confidentially to the extent reasonably possible and consistent with the legitimate objectives of the study.

Research outputs should preferably present results in aggregated, statistical, or otherwise appropriately de-identified form whenever individual identification is not necessary.

Uploaded microscope images should not be publicly distributed merely because they were submitted to the research system.

8. Research Participation Consent

Participation in research involving personal information should be voluntary when consent is the applicable basis for processing.

By providing research information and selecting the applicable consent option, the participant acknowledges that the information may be processed for the specified research purposes described in this notice.

Research Consent Statement

I understand that information and microscope images I voluntarily submit may be processed by MicroCount STEVision for the stated research purposes, including image analysis, particle quantification, research evaluation, and preparation of research outputs.

I understand that the results generated by MicroCount STEVision are intended for research purposes and that suspected particle detections are not automatically equivalent to laboratory-confirmed microplastics.

9. Rights of Data Subjects

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, data subjects have recognized rights concerning their personal information.

Right to Be Informed Users have the right to receive information about the collection and processing of their personal data.
Right to Access Users may have the right to access personal information concerning them, subject to applicable conditions.
Right to Correct Users may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information, subject to applicable requirements.
Right to Object Users may have the right to object to certain processing of their personal information.
Right to Erasure or Blocking In applicable circumstances, users may request erasure or blocking of personal information.
Right to Data Portability Where applicable, users may have the right to obtain their personal data in a structured and commonly used format.
Right to File a Complaint A data subject may lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when the requirements of the law are applicable.
Right to Damages The law provides a right to damages under applicable circumstances.

10. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Research information should not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals or organizations.

Where research information must be shared with authorized research personnel, institutions, reviewers, or other parties, the research team should ensure that the disclosure is consistent with the declared research purpose and applicable privacy requirements.

Where possible, research outputs should use aggregated or de-identified information rather than unnecessary identifying details.

11. Automated Image Analysis

MicroCount STEVision uses computational image-analysis procedures to examine microscope images and generate particle measurements.

Automated image analysis may identify suspected particle regions based on the analysis criteria implemented by the system.

Scientific limitation: A computational detection is a suspected particle measurement and should not automatically be interpreted as laboratory confirmation of polymer composition or human health risk.
