MicroCount STEVision

Seeing the Invisible Through Computational Microplastic Detection

MicroCount is a web-based computational system designed to detect, count, and assess suspected microplastic particles from microscope images of school drinking water samples. Using Fiji/ImageJ-assisted image analysis, the system supports research, environmental monitoring, and classroom-based scientific inquiry in Dasmariñas, Cavite.

Study Overview

A quantitative descriptive-comparative system for identifying microplastic particles in selected school drinking water sources.

Research Focus

This study examines microplastic contamination in drinking water from selected schools in Dasmariñas, Cavite using Fiji/ImageJ analysis and the MicroCount website as the computational screening tool.

Research Design

The project uses a quantitative descriptive-comparative approach to determine the presence, quantity, and visible characteristics of suspected microplastic particles in water samples.

Purpose

The platform helps researchers compare particle counts, classify contamination risk, and generate screening results that support scientific interpretation and school-based environmental awareness.
