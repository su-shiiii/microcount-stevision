MicroCount STEVision

Seeing the Invisible Through Computational Microplastic Detection

MicroCount is a web-based computational system designed to detect, count, and assess suspected microplastic particles from microscope images of school drinking water samples. Using Fiji/ImageJ-assisted image analysis, the system supports research, environmental monitoring, and classroom-based scientific inquiry in Dasmariñas, Cavite.
