Recommended Number of Images per Water Sample

Guidelines for improving the reliability and representativeness of MicroCount STEVision results.

Recommended Minimum

10+
Microscope Images per Water Sample

MicroCount STEVision recommends analyzing at least 10 microscope images from each water sample whenever practical. Analyzing multiple microscope fields provides more observations of the prepared sample than relying on a single image.

This recommendation is intended to improve the representativeness of the estimated average particle count and reduce the influence of an unusually high or low particle count in one microscope field.

Why Analyze Multiple Images?

Microplastic particles may not be distributed uniformly throughout a prepared water sample. Some microscope fields may contain many particles, while other fields may contain relatively few.

If only one or a small number of images are analyzed, the calculated result may be strongly influenced by the particular microscope fields selected.

Increasing the number of microscope images provides more observations and can make the estimated average particle count more stable and representative of the analyzed sample.

How the Number of Images Affects Reliability

Number of Images General Reliability Guideline
1–3 Low A small number of microscope fields may not adequately represent the variation within the prepared sample.
4–9 Moderate Provides additional observations, but particle distribution may still be insufficiently represented.
10 or more Recommended Provides a broader set of microscope fields for estimating the average observed particle count.
20 or more Higher observational coverage Provides additional observations and may improve the stability of the estimated average when particle distribution is highly variable.

MicroCount Average Calculation

MicroCount uses the number of analyzed images when calculating the average detected particle count.

Average Particles per Image

Total Detected Particles ÷ Number of Images Analyzed

For example, if 10 microscope images contain a combined total of 50 detected particles:

50 ÷ 10 = 5 particles/image

The resulting average represents the average number of suspected particles observed per microscope image.

Recommended Procedure

  1. Prepare the water sample using the same procedure for each sample.
  2. Capture microscope images from different representative fields of the prepared sample.
  3. Analyze at least 10 microscope images whenever practical.
  4. Record the number of suspected particles detected in each image.
  5. Calculate the average number of detected particles per image.
  6. Report the total number of images analyzed together with the particle count and calculated average.

Scientific Basis and Limitation

The recommendation of 10 or more images is a MicroCount study-design recommendation intended to increase observational coverage and improve the representativeness of the estimated average.

It should not be described as a universal international minimum requirement for all microplastic studies. The appropriate number of images can depend on the study objectives, sample preparation, particle distribution, microscope method, and other analytical conditions.

Increasing the number of images does not by itself confirm that every detected particle is plastic. MicroCount results should therefore be described as suspected microplastic particles unless an appropriate confirmatory method is used.
