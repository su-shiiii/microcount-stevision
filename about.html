About MicroCount STEVision

Empowering Student Researchers Through Computational Image Analysis

📖 Research Overview

MicroCount STEVision is a web-based platform developed to estimate suspected microplastic particles from microscope images of drinking water samples using Fiji/ImageJ-assisted computational image analysis.

The system provides a simple, accessible, and low-cost screening tool that supports environmental monitoring, scientific research, and student-led investigations.

🎯 Research Objectives

✔ Detect suspected microplastic particles from microscope images.
✔ Estimate particle counts using Fiji/ImageJ.
✔ Classify contamination into different risk levels.
✔ Support environmental monitoring in schools.
✔ Generate organized research reports.

📖 About the Study

Seeing the Invisible: A Computational Analysis of Microplastics in School Drinking Water in Dasmariñas, Cavite

This research was conducted under the Special Program in Science, Technology and Engineering (STE) at Dasmariñas East Integrated High School during Academic Year 2026–2027.

🤝 Acknowledgements

This platform was developed to support Filipino student researchers conducting environmental studies involving drinking water quality and computational image analysis. We sincerely acknowledge the guidance of our research coach and the support of our school throughout the completion of this study.

⚠ Important Disclaimer

The risk assessment presented by MicroCount is an interpretative classification adapted from Li & Xing (2025). The website analyzes suspected microplastic particle counts obtained through computational image processing and does not directly measure microplastic concentration in μm/mL.


The displayed risk level serves as an interpretation framework based on the adopted toxicity scale. Results should be considered preliminary screening data and may require verification through laboratory methods for definitive contamination assessment.
